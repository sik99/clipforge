# ClipForge

ClipForge converts uploaded source videos into output videos according to user-selected conversion options.

## Language

**Job**:
A unit of requested work covering upload of a source video and its conversion.

**Abandoned job**:
A job that ended because no complete source video was uploaded within the allowed waiting period. It no longer accepts uploads.

**Conversion**:
The processing of a source video into an output video according to committed conversion options. Conversion is one stage of a job.

**Converted result**:
The output video made available after a successful conversion.

**Result expiration**:
The end of a converted result's availability for new downloads, distinct from physical deletion of the output video.
